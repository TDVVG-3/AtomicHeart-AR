# In-game checklist - cycle 012 (carrier p7)

You do NOT need any tools for this. Just play and watch. A phone photo of anything odd is enough.

How to make sure the mod is the installed one: the game language stays **Korean** (do not change it). The mod
replaces the Korean text. Nothing else needs to be done.

Start: **New game** (or the earliest save you have, on the boat / before the first speech).

## 1. The opening (most important)

- Start a new game and let the opening narration play.
- **Q1:** Is the line about the Chelomey complex (built "from the ground up to be the ideal comfortable
  workplace") now in **Arabic**? It was English before.
- **Q2:** Do the two speakers in the boat scene (the sarcastic soldier and the talking glove) sound natural
  in Arabic? The glove should be pompous and the soldier sarcastic.
- **Q3:** Is the speaker name above the subtitles still `[ Char-les ]` in Latin? Write down exactly what you
  see in the brackets.
- **Q4:** Find a second speaker label (for example the computer/AI voice or Sechenov) and write down what its
  brackets show. This tells me whether the bracket labels translate at all in your build.

## 2. The first area (Chelomey)

Walk through the city and watch for these side conversations - all of them should now be Arabic:

- the fitness/aerobics broadcast on the loudspeaker (counting "one, two, three, four"),
- the child whose flying robot got stuck in a tree,
- the worker whose repair robots are misbehaving (the woman gives him a control code),
- the free-photo session (people posing, "say cheese", the atom and the statue in the frame),
- the two people on the bench reading a poem, then talking about the future,
- the kiosk advert (Infokiosk 55) and the newspaper questions,
- the phone prank calls,
- the speech announcements ("Sechenov's address will begin in ten minutes").

## 3. What to look for (things that could be wrong)

- **English text left over** - any sentence still in English.
- **Clipped text** - a label cut off with `...` or a word chopped inside a button.
- **Broken text** - square boxes, question marks, letters in the wrong order, or text running off the screen.
- **Wrong label** - a speaker name that does not match who is talking, or a name in Latin that should be
  Arabic (or the reverse).
- **A line shown in parts** - a long subtitle that stops before the end, or scrolls oddly.

## 4. What to send me

For each problem: the scene (where you were), what you expected, what you saw, and a photo if you can. If
everything is fine, just say so - "opening Arabic, labels Arabic, no problems" is a perfect report.
